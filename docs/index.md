# InboxPilot

Last updated: September 2026

InboxPilot is a personal email automation tool. It was built by Sourabh Saxena, who is also its only user. It is not offered to anyone else.

It sorts selected emails from the developer's own Gmail account, logs them to the developer's Notion workspace, adds dates and deadlines to the developer's Google Calendar, and sends alerts to the developer on Telegram.
